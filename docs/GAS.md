# Gas analysis

**Setup.** Measured on 2026-09-24 on a local nitro devnode:
- nitro-node v3.11.4, ArbOS 61, L1 price set to 0;
- both Stylus programs cached, as `scripts/deploy.sh` does on every network;
- traced with `debug_traceTransaction` (call tracer).

**What was measured.** Real transactions with real proofs:
- **Proofs:** UltraHonk proofs of WebAuthn P-256 assertions:
  - from the end-to-end tests (`packages/sdk/test/e2e`, bb.js 5.2.0 in Node);
  - from the app in headless Chrome.
- **Verifier:** generated by `bb write_solidity_verifier -t evm --optimized` with bb 5.2.0 (a ZK verifier).

## Before and after

| | bb default verifier | bb optimized verifier | + packed storage, cached programs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Toolchain | nargo 1.0.0-beta.22, bb 5.0.0-nightly.20260522 | nargo 1.0.0-beta.25, bb 5.2.0 | same |
| Solidity verifier | default generator (`-t evm`), with libraries | `-t evm --optimized`: one contract, 16,742 bytes deployed | same |
| Circuit | 81,605 gates (2^17) | 56,528 gates (2^16) | same |
| Proof | 9,152 bytes | 8,768 bytes | same |
| Proving in headless Chrome, 8 threads | ~2.1 s | 1.85 s (median of 3) | same |
| `modexp` calls in `verify` | 466 (1,882,820 gas) | 3 (12,144 gas) | same |
| `HonkVerifier.verify` | 3,781,398 | 712,554 | 712,554 |
| `pay` from the app to the demo merchant | 4,171,302 | 1,073,882 | 1,021,759 |
| That `pay` on Arbitrum One | ≈ $0.22 | ≈ $0.06 | ≈ $0.05 |
| `createAccount` | ~479,000 | ~479,000 | 383,296 |

**The last column also adds protections, and is still cheaper.** It adds:
- the new-recipient cap, the fee limit and the payment sheet check;
- the on-chain list of scheduled changes.

It is cheaper because of two changes:
- the account now packs small fields into shared storage slots: a payment updates its nonce and its spending window in one slot;
- both Stylus programs are cached, which cuts their per-call initialization cost.

The dollar figures use prices read on 2026-09-24:
- a 0.02 gwei gas price (`ArbGasInfo.getPricesInWei`);
- an ETH price of $2,668.

On Arbitrum One, the proof's calldata adds about $0.002 of L1 data fee (about 73 gwei per byte).

**Where the verifier savings came from:**
- **Smaller circuit.** bb 5.x checks secp256r1 signatures with fewer gates (81,605 down to 56,528). The circuit now fits in 2^16, so:
  - there is one less sumcheck round;
  - the proof is 384 bytes smaller;
  - proving is faster.
- **Batched inversions.** The `--optimized` generator batches field inversions, so `verify` makes 3 `modexp` calls instead of 466.
  - Most of the saving comes from this.
  - With bb 5.2.0 but the default generator, `verify` still costs 3,693,229 gas (455 `modexp` calls).

## What is left

- **Elliptic-curve precompiles** (`ecMul`, `ecAdd`, `ecPairing`): about 463,550 gas, 65% of `verify`.
  - They have fixed prices, so moving the verifier to Stylus cannot make them cheaper.
- **Field arithmetic and the keccak transcript:** about 237k gas.
  - This is the part a Stylus verifier could still reduce.
- **Calldata:** about 144k gas for the 8,768-byte proof and the client data.
- **Account creation:** 383,296 gas. That covers a CREATE2 clone plus the first writes to ten storage slots.
  - A clone with immutable arguments would remove most of those writes.
- **Baseline for comparison:** a non-private passkey signature check through `P256VERIFY` costs 3,450 gas. The ZK proof costs about 200 times that.
